About Text Case Conversion

Text case conversion allows you to transform your text into different formats such as UPPER CASE, lower case, Title Case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, Sentence case, and Toggle Case. This is useful for developers, writers, and content creators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Toggle Case?
A: It switches uppercase letters to lowercase and vice versa.

Q: What is Sentence Case?
A: It capitalizes only the first letter of the sentence.
