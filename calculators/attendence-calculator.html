About Attendance Calculation

Attendance calculation helps students track their class attendance and plan accordingly. Most institutions require a minimum of 75% attendance. This tool helps you calculate your current percentage, find how many classes you need to attend, or determine how many you can safely miss while maintaining the required percentage.

Attendance Status Guide

Percentage Range Status Description
75% - 100% ✅ Good Meets minimum requirements
60% - 74% ⚠️ Warning Below minimum, action needed
0% - 59% ❌ Critical Seriously below requirements
