How Age is Calculated

Age is calculated by finding the difference between the reference date (usually today) and the birthdate. It considers years, months, and days for precise calculation.

Formula & Example

Formula:
Years = Reference Year - Birth Year (adjust if birthday not yet occurred)
Months = Reference Month - Birth Month (adjust if negative)
Days = Reference Day - Birth Day (adjust if negative)

Example: Birthdate: 15 March 2000, Reference Date: 11 September 2025
Age = 25 years, 5 months, 26 days
