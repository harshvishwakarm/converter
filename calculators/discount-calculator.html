About Discount Calculation

Discount calculation helps you find savings, final prices, and discount percentages. It's widely used in shopping, sales, business, and everyday financial decisions to determine the best deals and actual costs.

Formulas & Examples

1. Calculate Discount Amount

Formula: Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount %) ÷ 100

Example: Original Price = $100, Discount = 20% → ($100 × 20) ÷ 100 = $20 discount

2. Calculate Final Price

Formula: Final Price = Original Price - (Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100)

Example: Original = $100, Discount = 20% → $100 - ($100 × 20 ÷ 100) = $80

3. Find Original Price

Formula: Original Price = Final Price ÷ (1 - Discount % ÷ 100)

Example: Final Price = $80, Discount = 20% → $80 ÷ (1 - 20 ÷ 100) = $100

4. Find Discount Percentage

Formula: Discount % = ((Original Price - Final Price) ÷ Original Price) × 100

Example: Original = $100, Final = $80 → (($100 - $80) ÷ $100) × 100 = 20% discount
