About Percentage Calculation

Percentage is calculated by dividing the obtained marks by the total marks and multiplying by 100. It is widely used in exams, statistics, finance, and everyday life to show proportions.

Basic Percentage

Formula: Percentage = (Obtained ÷ Total) × 100

Example: Obtained = 450, Total = 500 → (450/500) × 100 = 90%

What Percent is X of Y?

Formula: Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: X = 25, Y = 200 → (25/200) × 100 = 12.5%

X% of What Number?

Formula: Total = (Part × 100) ÷ Percentage

Example: 20% of what is 40? → (40 × 100) ÷ 20 = 200

Percent Increase/Decrease

Formula: % Change = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

Example: From 50 to 75 → ((75−50)/50) × 100 = 50% increase
