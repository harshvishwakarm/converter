About Random Number Generator

The Random Number Generator helps you quickly generate a random integer between your chosen minimum and maximum values. This tool is useful for games, decision making, practice questions, and more. Just enter the range and click "Generate" to get your random number instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I generate negative numbers?
A: Yes, you can enter a negative value in the minimum or maximum field, and the tool will generate numbers accordingly.

Q: Will I get decimal numbers?
A: No, this tool generates only whole numbers (integers).
