About Word Counter

The Word Counter tool helps you quickly calculate the number of words, characters with spaces, characters without spaces, sentences, and paragraphs in your text. This is helpful for writers, bloggers, students, and professionals who need to stay within word limits or measure text length for SEO and readability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?
A: Characters with spaces count all letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. Characters without spaces ignore blank spaces.

Q: Does the tool count line breaks?
A: Line breaks are counted as paragraph separators, not characters.
