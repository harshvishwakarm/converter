About Number Conversion

Convert numbers into English words using Indian numbering system. Supports Hundred, Thousand, Lakh, and Crore. Useful for writing checks, invoices, or learning numbers.

FAQs

Q: Can I convert words back to numbers?

A: Yes, just click the swap button and enter words like "one lakh fifty thousand".

Q: What's the maximum number supported?

A: This converter supports numbers up to crores (10 million).
